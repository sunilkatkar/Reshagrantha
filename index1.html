Our story

FIG. 01 — Site study, ongoing residential commission

Spaces that are timeless, meaningful, and in harmony with their environment.

Reshagrantha is an award-winning architectural firm recognised for its creative excellence and diverse portfolio spanning architecture, interior, landscape, architectural conservation, art conservation, metal conservation and artwork projects.

Led by our Principal Architect — honoured as India's Most Promising and Trendsetting Architect of 2022 — the studio embodies a vision rooted in integrity, innovation and contextual sensitivity. Each project reflects a dialogue between tradition and modernity, precision and creativity.

Guided by nature-centric design ethics, we build lasting relationships and designs that celebrate life, culture and coexistence with nature.
