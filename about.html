Our story
Every site already has a logic — of light, land and lineage. Our work begins by listening to it.
Ar. Aniket Shendge Founder & Principal Architect

Founded in 2015 and led by a Principal Architect honoured as India’s Most Promising and Trendsetting Architect of 2022, the studio embodies a vision rooted in integrity, innovation and contextual sensitivity.

Guided by nature-centric design ethics, Reshagrantha creates spaces that are timeless, meaningful and in harmony with their environment. Each project reflects a dialogue between tradition and modernity, precision and creativity.

With an unwavering commitment to quality, detail and originality, the firm builds lasting relationships and designs that celebrate life, culture and coexistence with nature.
