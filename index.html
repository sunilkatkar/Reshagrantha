Our story
Every site already has a logic — of light, land and lineage. Our work begins by listening to it.

Reshagrantha is an award-winning architectural practice recognised for its creative excellence and a portfolio spanning architecture, interiors, landscape, architectural conservation, art and metal conservation.

Guided by nature-centric design ethics, we create spaces that are timeless, meaningful and in harmony with their environment — a dialogue between tradition and modernity, precision and creativity.

Ar. Aniket Shendge Founder & Principal Architect

Our services
01

Architecture

We experiment with structural form, material and construction technique in a cost-effective manner — resolving each brief against its climate, site and budget.

02

Interiors

Your own space, personalised to your comfort and our vision — detailed down to joinery, lighting and the commissioned artwork that completes a room.

03

Landscape

Planning, design, management and nurturing of built and natural environments to create and enable life between the buildings.

04

Architectural Conservation

We retain the authentic built form of older buildings through considered restoration, serving the long-term preservation of structures and the craft traditions they carry.

Heritage practice

Temples, forts and the craft of keeping them standing.

Led by a Principal Architect with an M.Arch in Conservation Architecture and empanelled with the State Archaeology and Museums Department, Government of Maharashtra — a practice few studios in the region are qualified to undertake.
